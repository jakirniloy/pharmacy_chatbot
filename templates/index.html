When to seek emergency room (ER) care immediately

Call 911 / 999 / Local Emergency Services Immediately

Do NOT wait for a chatbot response if you or someone else experiences severe symptoms.

  • Chest pain, severe pressure, or pain radiating to jaw or left arm
  • Severe difficulty breathing, choking, or wheezing
  • Signs of stroke: Facial drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech (F.A.S.T.)
  • Severe allergic reaction (Anaphylaxis): Swelling of lips, tongue, or throat
  • Unconsciousness, fainting, or sudden unresponsiveness
  • Poisoning or drug overdose: Contact National Poison Control immediately
  • Uncontrolled profuse bleeding
